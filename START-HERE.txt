MAKE THE NIMBUS OS LINUX ISO WITHOUT INSTALLING ANYTHING (uses GitHub's free computers)

1. Go to github.com and sign in (make a free account if needed).
2. Click  +  (top right) > New repository. Name it nimbus-os. Choose PUBLIC. Click Create repository.
3. On the new page click "uploading an existing file".
4. Open this folder on your PC, press Ctrl+A to select EVERYTHING in it (including the ".github" folder),
   and drag it all into the GitHub page. Wait until the upload finishes, then click Commit changes.
   (If ".github" does not show in the list, upload it separately by dragging that folder alone.)
5. Click the Actions tab. If asked, click "I understand my workflows, go ahead and enable them".
6. Click "Build Nimbus OS ISO" on the left, then Run workflow > Run workflow.
7. Wait about 30-60 minutes. Open the finished run (green tick) and, at the bottom, click
   "nimbus-os-linux-iso" under Artifacts. You get a zip with the ISO inside.
8. When you have the ISO, you can delete the repository: Settings > scroll to the bottom > Delete this repository.

Tick "Lite build" in step 6 for a smaller ISO without Wine (Windows apps will not run).
If the run fails (red cross), click it, open the failed step and send the last 20 lines to Claude.
